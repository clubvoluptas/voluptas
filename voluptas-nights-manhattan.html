THE ROOM

Every couple in the room was chosen.

Each one a member of the Society, each one introduced by hand. Couples who carry themselves the way you do, already in the room when you step off the elevator. Music moving, glasses cold. All you do is walk in.

THE NIGHT

One address. Five ways to lose the hour.

I · THE UNDERCURRENT

Something in the air

Moments of provocation, threaded through the night and announced to no one. You'll know when the room goes quiet.

II · THE PRIVATE ROOMS

Behind the velvet

Tucked away from the dance floor, curtained rooms for the two of you. Draw the velvet. The rest of the room carries on without you. Return flushed, or take your time.

III · THE BAR

Civilized spirits

Champagne, proper cocktails, your usual. Premium and poured freely from nine until one while the room warms around you.

IV · THE FLOOR

Dance with your spouse. At first.

By midnight the floor belongs to everyone. The woman you met that afternoon. The couple you've been writing to all month. Partners change as the hour gets later.

V · THE ADDRESS

Straight up, out of sight

A private elevator from the street to our door. Art Deco rooms, warm brass, low light. Ours alone until two.

THE CITY
