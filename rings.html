Your first payment comes on day eight. In the Inner Ring, your rate then holds for as long as you stay.

  • The included Voluptas Night is yours in any city within your year, and closes at renewal.
  • Booking it uses a private code, sent with your first annual payment. A Voluptas Night booked and missed counts as used.
  • The year is non-refundable once your Voluptas Night is booked.
  • Voluptas Nights are priced by venue. The Inner Ring's 10% applies to every one.
  • Weekend and Deeper Experiences are reserved and priced separately. The Inner Ring holds the Invitation.
  • Discretion is the condition of entry. Every member has signed a nondisclosure agreement.
