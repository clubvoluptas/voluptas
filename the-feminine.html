Last summer

Nobody came home the same.

A group of women took fifteen acres in the Montana mountains for themselves. A few words from what they wrote afterwards.

I didn’t expect to come home feeling like I’d met a version of myself I hadn’t given enough room to exist before.

“My cheeks hurting from laughing, my eyes red from crying, and my heart happy.”

“I don’t have to ask permission from anyone but myself to experience pleasure.”

“When I was shown one of myself, I didn’t believe it was me at first glance.”

Some of them are coming back. There is room for you beside them.

The Property

Your own cabin.
Your own fire.
Your door.

Warm wood walls. A wood burning fireplace that takes the chill off the mountain nights. Windows full of forest. A king bed dressed in linens someone thought about before you arrived.

And on your private deck, your own hot tub. Not a shared amenity. Not down a path somewhere. Yours. Waiting.

Fifteen private acres. Nine cabins. One group of women who know exactly why they are here. What you do behind your door is yours alone.

Arrival

Someone has already been here.

Something soft, folded and tied in silk ribbon. A small bag of things chosen for you and nobody else. Dried flowers from the meadow. Champagne already cold.

And the shape of the next four days on a single card, waiting to be read with your first glass.

A few of the evenings are not explained. That is on purpose.

What we have planned for you.

i. The Nordic Cabins

Fifteen private acres taken entirely for ourselves. Your own cabin. Your own fire. Your own deck. A steam barrel sauna under the Montana sky. An infrared sauna for the mornings you want the heat without the ceremony. A communal fire that nobody puts out early. The Milky Way directly above your hot tub on a Friday night.

ii. The Sanctuary

A private wellness sanctuary bought out entirely for one day, for one group of women. The thermal circuit. Red light. A vibroacoustic bed that does things to your nervous system you will spend the drive home trying to describe to your husband. Private bodywork. Breathwork. A traditional Finnish sauna…no clothes required, where curiosity becomes a little more…embodied.

iii. The Table

Welcome champagne poured the moment you arrive. A private chef who understands that food is not fuel, it is ceremony. Farm to market dinners under the open Montana sky. Espresso martinis by the fire. Brunch on the mountain while the forest watches. A long table where the wine keeps appearing and nobody checks the time.

iv. The Town

One evening we leave the cabins for the kind of dinner that could only happen in a town this beautiful and this unknown. Open sky. Cocktails someone thought about. A table set just for us, where the conversation goes exactly where it needs to go. Then back to the cabins. Back to the fire. Back to the stars.

Having this experience on my own made me miss him deeply.

Your husband will notice when you get home.

This is not for everyone.

The Feminine is a private gathering of women who are happily married and still hungry. Who chose their husbands and choose them still. Who understand that desire is not a problem to be managed but a fire to be fed. That combination is rarer than anyone admits and nearly impossible to find on an ordinary girls weekend.

Some of you were on the mountain last summer. Some of you have been reading their words and wondering what it would feel like to be in that room. Both of you belong here.

She is carrying something quietly. A curiosity. A version of herself she has not fully introduced to anyone yet. A feeling she cannot always name but recognizes immediately when she reads certain words.

Every woman here has been curated. Every woman here lives in the same world you do. Nothing you feel here requires an explanation. Nothing you discover here requires an apology.

The walls do not talk. The mountain watches and says nothing.

After dark.

And then there are the nights. The fire burning low. Steam rising off your hot tub into the stars. A robe that does not stay on long. Women who have stopped pretending they came only for the sauna.

Some of you have wondered what it would be like to kiss a woman. Some of you already know.

Whatever happens after dark happens because you wanted it to.

Nothing is expected.
Everything is permitted.

Everything is included.

You arrive. You exhale. Someone else has thought of everything.

There is something that happens when a woman spends four days completely in herself. Unhurried. Seen. Alive in her body in ways she forgot were available to her. She does not leave that in the mountains. She carries it home. Her husband feels it before she says a word. The way she moves. The way she looks at him. The particular quality of her attention when she has recently been reminded of who she is.

That is not a side effect of this experience. That is the whole point.
