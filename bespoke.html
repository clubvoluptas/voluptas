01Paris · 11:40 P.M.

The Rain

It has been raining since dinner. She is at the end of the bar in the black dress you chose, alone, a little longer than she should be. The man who takes the seat beside her speaks to her only in French. She understands more than she lets on.

You are in the corner banquette. Close enough to see her laugh. Too far to hear what made her. The bartender already knows your drink.

The Detail

We can't promise the rain. So we hold the room for three nights.

Anyone can book the suite. Very few can fill it.

02Lisbon · After Dinner

Her Turn

She has wondered about a woman for years. She may never have said it out loud, not even to you.

An apartment above the river. Candles, open windows, a record she loves already playing. The woman waiting there is elegant, unhurried, and entirely sure of herself. You may stay in the room, or you may hear about it later. That part is hers to decide.

The Detail

She learns the woman's first name at the door. Her last name, never.

03New York · 9:00 P.M.

Strangers

A courier delivers two sealed envelopes. Inside each, a name, a profession, a past, and the address of a hotel bar. Neither of you reads the other's.

At nine o'clock someone sits down beside you and asks if the seat is taken. You have never met. You will spend the whole night finding out who they are, and you will not break character until morning.

The Detail

The two stories are written to collide. One of them holds a secret about the other.

04Montana · Snowbound

The Stranger

A cabin above a frozen lake. A fire already lit, two feet of fresh snow, and the road closed until morning.

Near midnight, a knock. His truck went into the ditch a mile down. He is cold, polite, and staying the night. There is only one fire.

The Detail

The snow is real. The ditch is not.

Our Only Rule

Details
matter.

Fantasies rarely fall apart on the big things. They fall apart on the small ones. The small ones are the whole job.

  1. 01The song that is playing as she walks in.
  2. 02Which side of the bar she sits on, and why.
  3. 03The name on the reservation. It isn't yours.
  4. 04The perfume on the pillow. Not hers.
  5. 05The driver who never looks in the mirror.
  6. 06How cold the champagne is at one in the morning.
  7. 07A note on the pillow, in handwriting she doesn't recognize.
  8. 08The exit, planned before the entrance.

You will notice almost none of this. That is how you'll know it worked.

The Commission

How it unfolds.

I

A private call with the two of you, together and then, if you wish, apart. We listen to what you say, and to what you keep circling.

We are there, unseen, until the moment we are no longer needed. Then we are gone.
