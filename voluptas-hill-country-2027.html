The main house

Nine suites occupy the estate's main house — a sprawling stone and timber structure perched above the cedar-covered valley, with a great room, terrace, pool, and sauna all set against the Hill Country horizon.

The gathering space

Built to hold a room full of people who have something to say.

The great room spans the full width of the main house — stone fireplace on one wall, floor-to-ceiling windows to the terrace on the other. Mornings begin here. Nights end here.

The terrace opens onto the pool and hot tub, with the valley and the horizon unobstructed beyond. At dusk, the estate lighting transforms the grounds into something you won't be in a hurry to leave.

The Casita House

Separate from the main house, a detached stone cottage sits further along the ridge — simpler in its finishes, quieter in its setting, with the same Hill Country horizon and two suites of its own. The Casitas share a bath and have no connection to the main house's common areas. They are for couples who want something apart.
