Naples

Florida  ·  February 14–17

The Town

Naples does not try.
It doesn’t have to.

Old Florida elegance, quietly polished. Quiet streets under the banyans. Boutiques on Third Street South. A Fifth Avenue where the patios fill at golden hour and everyone is dressed a little better than they need to be.

White sand beaches with nobody shouting on them. Sunsets off the pier that people move here for. It is the kind of town that never raises its voice, because it has never needed to.

We will take one evening in town, dressed for it. The rest of the time, Naples can wait. The estate is better.

The Estate

Two and a half tropical acres.
A waterfall.
No one else.

Nine thousand square feet of glass, art, and air. Twenty five foot windows pour the Florida light into the great room. More than ninety works of art on the walls. A chef’s kitchen that will keep producing things you swore you would not eat.

Outside, a 150 foot heated saltwater pool runs the length of the grounds. A twenty foot rain curtain waterfall pours into it. A moat curls around the house. The hot tub seats fourteen, which means it seats all of us at once, champagne included.

Across the lawn, a Bali inspired guest house with its own hot tub and a wraparound porch that looks straight into the falling water.

What we have planned for you.

i. The Estate

The whole property, gated, for four days. Mornings by the pool before anyone else is awake. Clean modern architecture, walls of glass, and art at every turn, all of it softened by tropical green. The nights, and whatever you make of them, where the conversation goes somewhere it would never go at brunch.

ii. The Ritual

A full spa day without ever leaving the estate. Treatments brought to us, poolside and in the shade of the palms. Slow yoga on the lawn, sun already warm on your shoulders. The kind of care you spend the other fifty one weeks of the year giving to everyone else.

iii. The Bloom

One afternoon given over entirely to being a woman. Flowers by the armful, or fine china and a proper tea we dress up for. Something soft and lovely, slowed all the way down, with someone there to capture it. Details are still blooming. The feeling is already decided.

iv. The Table

A private chef at the estate. Espresso martinis, because it is Valentine’s. Long lunches in the shade. And one evening out on Fifth Avenue South, dressed for it, at a table where the cocktails are thoughtful and nobody asks you to be anyone but who you are this weekend.
