The Deeper Experience

This is four nights inside a private luxury estate in the Texas Hill Country, reserved exclusively for eleven extraordinary married couples who are deeply committed to each other and ready to find out how much further they can go.

Your private world. No agenda. No outside noise. Nowhere you need to be except here.

This is not a retreat. Not a feelings circle. Not a tantra workshop.

Eleven private ensuite rooms. Stone terraces with fire pits overlooking the hills. A pool. Cold plunge. Hot tub. Sauna. A formal dining room where the chef brings the meal to you every evening.

The property is yours entirely. No other guests. No outside world. All just forty minutes from Austin International Airport.

For Her

Your
Secret.

Being fully seen as a sexual woman and choosing to step toward it instead of away.

Four days of that feeling building, deepening, going further than a single evening ever could. Four days of being a woman with a secret life with her husband that the dinner party, the gym, the perfectly curated outside world knows nothing about.

You will return to your husband carrying something new in your body. He will see it in your face the moment you walk back into the room. He will not be able to look away from you for the rest of the weekend. He will not want to.

This is the erotic field. Couples moving through their own explorations around you, beside you, in view of you, on soft cushions and draped fabrics, in corners and doorways, in the warm half-dark of a Hill Country night. You decide what you want to witness. You decide what you want to feel. You decide how far your evening goes with your husband inside that charged, beautiful space.

Nobody is watching you to see what you will choose. Nobody is keeping score. The only thing in the room is desire, yours, his, everyone's, and the extraordinary relief of finally being somewhere it is allowed to exist.

By Sunday morning you will feel things you have always known were possible but never had the right room for.

And you will feel, in the most alive version of yourself, safe.

Friday · Late

The Separation. Then the Reunion.

The women gather. The men gather. Separate experiences, each profound, each activating in ways that are impossible to fully prepare for. You return to each other changed. A slow, civilized morning. Cappuccinos, lovemaking, the hot tub. Optional Saturday morning sensual movement. Lunch. Then the afternoon is yours. The pool. The fire pit. The cold plunge. Sunbathing, clothed or not, on private grounds where nobody can see in and nobody is keeping track. Your room. For those who have reserved one of the private experiences, an erotic couples massage, a photography session, Saturday afternoon is when that happens.

The anticipation of Saturday night is its own experience.

Saturday · Evening

The Dinner

A 3-course formal dinner prepared by your private chef, locally sourced ingredients, a fully custom menu built for the occasion, a dedicated server, the full group at the table together one last time before the evening opens.

You will dress for it. You will linger over it. The wine will be exactly right.

Every couple at that table knows what comes next.

Saturday · Late

The Crescendo

The evening opens with intention: frameworks for desire, for communication, for what becomes possible when a room full of committed couples gives each other permission to be fully alive.

Then the room changes.

Candles on every surface. Soft pillows and blankets arranged in a circle. Sensory tools laid out for couples to explore, rope, silk, warmth, texture, things you have never thought to reach for.

Then the facilitators and their partners take the center of that circle.

You watch. The room watches. And something that has been building since Thursday finally has nowhere left to go but here.

From there the evening belongs to everyone in it. Some find each other in ways they never have before. The facilitators remain present, available, active guides for couples who want to go further than they know how to go alone.

Sunday · Afterglow

The World Can Wait

No agenda. An elaborate, celebratory champagne brunch appears mid-morning. The pool is warm. The sauna is hot. By Sunday afternoon couples who were strangers Thursday are sitting together in the comfortable silence that takes most people years to earn.

For those who need to be home Sunday, brunch is a graceful exit. The weekend is complete. Nothing is missing.

For those who can stay, Sunday is yours entirely. A private massage in your room. A boudoir session if you booked one. A drive into Austin for dinner, just the two of you, or with another couple you have decided you are not ready to say goodbye to yet. No schedule. No facilitation. Just the afterglow and whatever you want to do inside it.
